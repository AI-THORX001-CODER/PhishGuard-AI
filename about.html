FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does PhishGuard AI store or send my URLs anywhere?

No — every scan runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and your scan history is saved only in this browser's local storage.

How accurate is the detection?

This demo uses a rule-based heuristic — domain structure, keywords, protocol, and similar signals — rather than a trained model, so treat each verdict as a helpful signal, not a guarantee. Always be cautious with any link that asks for credentials or payment details.

Can I clear my scan history?

Yes. Open the History page and use "Clear history" — you'll be asked to confirm since it can't be undone. You can also delete individual scans one at a time.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — the scanner, history, and this page are all fully responsive and work on phones and tablets.

Is this affiliated with any company mentioned in example URLs?

No. PhishGuard AI is an independent demo project and isn't affiliated with Google, PayPal, or any other brand referenced in its example URLs.
